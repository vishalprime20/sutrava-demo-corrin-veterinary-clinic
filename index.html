Care lanes

Companion animals and equine cases under one roof.

Corrin is a mixed practice: pets in the clinic and horses when yards need veterinary attention — including emergency equine care and pre-sale vetting.

  • Companion animals

    Medicine, surgery, and diagnostics for companion animals — the day-to-day care local pet owners bring to Fermoy.

  • Equine

    Equine emergency care, lameness exams, shockwave treatment, and pre-sale vetting for horses in the Corrin catchment.

The practice

A Fermoy clinic led by the people who see your animals.
